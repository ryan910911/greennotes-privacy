隱私權政策

最後更新：2026 年 11 月 17 日

這是「剩幾天－你的超級效期助理」（各地 App Store 上的名稱可能不同，例如 FreshLeft、のこり日数，以下稱本 App）的隱私權政策。簡單說：沒有廣告、沒有追蹤。不登入時，所有資料只存在你的手機裡；登入後，庫存會備份到雲端，只有你和你邀請的家人看得到。

不登入時

庫存品項、數量、位置、到期日、開封日、購物清單、照片和提醒設定，全部只存在你的裝置上，我們看不到。

掃條碼時，本 App 會用條碼號碼去查詢共用商品庫與 Open Food Facts（公開的食品資料庫），只送出條碼號碼本身。我們另外統計「每天掃到幾次、查不到的條碼號碼」來補齊商品資料，這份統計不含任何能認出你是誰的資訊。

登入並同步時

如果你選擇登入（「使用 Apple 登入」），我們會儲存：

  • 帳號資料：Email（若你在 Apple 登入時選擇隱藏 Email，就只會是 Apple 的轉寄地址）與帳號編號。
  • 你的庫存：品名、數量、位置、到期日、開封日與購物清單。
  • 家人共用：你建立的家庭、加入的成員與邀請碼。
  • 共用商品庫：你登錄過的商品「條碼、品名、常見保存天數」。這些會讓其他使用者掃到同一個條碼時直接帶出品名，但不會顯示是誰提供的。你的數量、位置、到期日等庫存資料不會進共用商品庫。

照片不會上傳，只存在你的裝置上。發票、收據與截圖的文字辨識完全在手機上進行，不會上傳。

這些資料存放在 Supabase 的伺服器上（位於日本東京），傳輸時加密。我們只用這些資料來提供備份、同步、家人共用與共用商品庫，不做其他用途，不出售、不分享給任何人。

共用商品庫的內容

共用商品庫的品名是使用者自己輸入的。含網址、電話、不雅或廣告字眼的品名不會被收錄；被多人回報有誤的品名會先隱藏。看到錯誤的品名，可以在加入庫存的畫面按「這個品名是錯的，回報」，或寫信給我們。

購買

付款完全由 Apple 處理，我們拿不到你的付款資料。

相機、照片與通知

只有在你掃條碼、拍照或從相簿挑照片時，本 App 才會使用相機或讀取那幾張照片；做分享圖時，只會在你按下儲存後把那一張圖存進相簿。到期提醒使用手機的本機通知。

追蹤與廣告

本 App 沒有廣告，不使用任何追蹤、分析或第三方資料收集工具。

保存與刪除

你可以隨時在 App 裡刪除任何品項。在「設定」裡可以退出家人共用或永久刪除帳號；刪除帳號時，雲端上你的帳號、庫存與你提供給共用商品庫的資料會一起刪除，用 Apple 登入的帳號也會一併撤銷 Apple 的登入授權。如果你是家庭裡最後一位成員，這個家庭的庫存也會刪除；還有其他成員時，共用的清單會留給他們。刪除 App 時，手機上的資料也會一起移除。

你的權利

你可以要求查閱、更正、匯出（設定裡有匯出 CSV）或刪除你的資料，也可以向你所在地的個資主管機關申訴。若你在歐盟，我們處理資料的依據是為了提供你要求的服務（GDPR 第 6 條第 1 項 b 款）。

兒童

本 App 不是專為兒童設計，我們不會刻意蒐集兒童的個人資料。

變更

政策有任何變更，會更新這一頁並修改上方的日期。

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

This policy covers the expiry-date tracker 剩幾天 (listed under local names such as "FreshLeft", "のこり日数", "유통기한 며칠" and "剩几天"). In short: no ads and no tracking. If you don't sign in, everything stays on your phone. If you sign in, your inventory is backed up to the cloud and only you and the family members you invite can see it.

Without signing in

Items, quantities, locations, expiry and opened dates, shopping list, photos and reminder settings are stored only on your device. We cannot see them.

When you scan a barcode, the app looks the number up in our shared product catalog and in Open Food Facts (a public food database); only the barcode number is sent. We also count daily scans and barcodes that were not found, to fill gaps in the catalog. These counts contain nothing that identifies you.

When you sign in to sync

If you sign in (Sign in with Apple), we store:

  • Account data: your email address (an Apple relay address if you chose to hide it) and an account ID.
  • Your inventory: item names, quantities, locations, expiry and opened dates, and your shopping list.
  • Family sharing: the family you create, its members and invite codes.
  • Shared product catalog: the barcode, product name and typical shelf life of products you register. Other users who scan the same barcode see the name, but never who provided it. Your quantities, locations and dates never go into the shared catalog.

Photos are not uploaded. Text recognition on receipts and screenshots runs entirely on your phone.

This data is stored with Supabase in Tokyo, Japan and encrypted in transit. We use it only for backup, sync, family sharing and the shared catalog. We never sell or share it.

User-entered product names

Names containing links, phone numbers, profanity or advertising are rejected, and names reported as wrong by several users are hidden. You can report a wrong name from the add-item screen or by emailing us.

Purchases

Payments are handled entirely by Apple. We never receive your payment details.

Camera, photos and notifications

The camera and photo library are used only when you scan, take a photo or pick one. A share card is saved to your library only when you tap save. Expiry reminders are local notifications.

Tracking and ads

No ads, no tracking, no analytics or third-party data collection SDKs.

Retention and deletion

You can delete any item at any time. In Settings you can leave family sharing or permanently delete your account. Deleting your account removes your account, cloud inventory and catalog contributions, and revokes Sign in with Apple. If you are the last member of a family, its inventory is deleted too; otherwise the shared list stays with the remaining members. Deleting the app removes the data on your phone.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction, export (CSV export is in Settings) or deletion of your data, and you may complain to your local data protection authority. For users in the EU, the legal basis is performance of the service you requested (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).

Children

The app is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect children's personal data.

Changes

We will update this page and the date above if this policy changes.
