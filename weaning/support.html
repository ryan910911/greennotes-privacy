副食品手帳 使用說明

有問題、建議，或發現食材資料有誤，請寫信到 ryan910911@gmail.com，通常幾天內回覆。

常見問題

一定要登入嗎？
不用。記一餐、過敏原追蹤、觀察期提醒都不用登入，資料只存在手機裡。

怎麼和家人一起記？
買斷後在「設定 → 家人共享」用 Apple 登入並產生邀請碼；家人用同一個 App 登入、輸入邀請碼就會看到同一份紀錄。用邀請碼加入是免費的。

免費版和買斷版差在哪？
記一餐、反應、過敏原與觀察期提醒永遠免費。一次買斷（不是訂閱）解鎖家人共享與雲端同步、多個寶寶、醫師 PDF 與 CSV、全部食譜和主畫面小工具。買斷可以透過 Apple 的「家人共享」分享給家庭成員。

換手機或重裝後付費不見了？
用同一個 Apple ID 打開 App 會自動恢復；也可以在付費畫面最下面按「恢復購買」。

沒收到觀察期提醒？
請到 iPhone「設定 → 通知」確認已允許這個 App 的通知。

怎麼刪除帳號？
「設定 → 家人共享 → 刪除帳號」。雲端資料會全部刪除，無法復原；需要的話請先在設定做一份備份檔。

本 App 是紀錄工具，不提供醫療建議。寶寶什麼時候開始吃副食品、先吃什麼，請問兒科醫師。

Solids Log – Support

Questions, suggestions or a wrong food entry? Email ryan910911@gmail.com. We usually reply within a few days.

Do I need an account? No. Logging meals, allergen tracking and watch-period reminders work without signing in; your records stay on your phone.

Family sharing: after unlocking, sign in with Apple in Settings → Family sharing and create an invite code. Family members sign in and enter the code to see the same log. Joining with an invite code is free.

Free vs. unlock: logging meals and reactions, allergens and watch-period reminders are always free. A one-time purchase (not a subscription) unlocks family sharing and cloud sync, more babies, the doctor PDF and CSV, all recipes and the Home Screen widget. The purchase can be shared through Apple Family Sharing.

Purchase missing after a new phone? It restores automatically on the same Apple ID, or tap Restore Purchases at the bottom of the unlock screen.

Delete account: Settings → Family sharing → Delete account. Cloud data is removed permanently; make a backup file in Settings first if you need one.

This app is a log, not medical advice. Ask your pediatrician when and how to start solids.
