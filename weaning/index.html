隱私權政策

最後更新：2026 年 11 月 17 日

這是「副食品手帳」（各地 App Store 上的名稱可能不同，以下稱本 App）的隱私權政策。簡單說：沒有廣告、沒有追蹤。不登入時，所有紀錄只存在你的手機裡；登入後，紀錄會備份到雲端，只有你和你邀請的家人看得到。

不登入時

寶寶資料、餵食紀錄、反應、照片和提醒設定，全部只存在你的裝置上，我們看不到。

登入並同步時

如果你選擇登入（「使用 Apple 登入」），我們會儲存：

  • 帳號資料：Email（若你在 Apple 登入時選擇隱藏 Email，就只會是 Apple 的轉寄地址）與帳號編號。
  • 你記的內容：寶寶名字與生日、自訂食材、每一餐的時間、食材、質地、份量、反應與備註。
  • 家庭成員：你建立的家庭、加入的成員與邀請碼。

照片不會上傳，只存在你的裝置上。

這些資料存放在 Supabase 的伺服器上（位於歐盟德國法蘭克福），傳輸時加密。只有你和你邀請進同一個家庭的人看得到。我們只用這些資料來提供同步與家人共享，不做其他用途，不出售、不分享給任何人。

購買

付款完全由 Apple 處理，我們拿不到你的付款資料。

相機、照片與通知

只有在你自己拍照或從相簿挑照片時，本 App 才會使用相機或讀取那幾張照片。照片會縮小後存在本 App 裡。如果開啟「從照片辨識食材」，辨識完全在手機上進行，不會上傳。提醒使用手機的本機通知。

追蹤與廣告

本 App 沒有廣告，不使用任何追蹤、分析或第三方資料收集工具。

保存與刪除

你可以隨時在 App 裡刪除任何紀錄。在「設定」裡可以退出家庭或刪除帳號，刪除帳號時，雲端上你的帳號資料會一起刪除。如果你是家庭裡最後一位成員，這個家庭的紀錄也會刪除。刪除 App 時，手機上的資料也會一起移除。

你的權利

你可以要求查閱、更正、匯出或刪除你的資料，也可以向你所在地的個資主管機關申訴。若你在歐盟、歐洲經濟區或英國，我們處理資料的依據是為了提供你要求的服務（GDPR 第 6 條第 1 項 b 款）。你記下的反應、症狀和備註可能屬於寶寶的健康資料；這部分我們只在你自己選擇登入並同步時，依你的明確同意處理（GDPR 第 9 條第 2 項 a 款），你可以隨時刪除那些紀錄或刪除帳號來撤回同意，不影響撤回前的處理。刪除帳號時，我們也會撤銷「使用 Apple 登入」的授權。

兒童

本 App 是給家長和照顧者用的，不是給兒童用的。寶寶的資料由家長自己輸入和管理。

變更

政策有任何變更，會更新這一頁並修改上方的日期。

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

This policy covers the baby-food log app 副食品手帳 (listed under local names such as "Solids Log", "Weaning Log", "Beikostbuch", "Mes purées", "Mis papillas", "Papinhas" and "離乳食手帳"). In short: no ads and no tracking. If you don't sign in, everything stays on your phone. If you sign in, your records are backed up to the cloud and only you and the family members you invite can see them.

Without signing in

Baby profiles, meals, reactions, photos and reminders are stored only on your device. We cannot see them.

When you sign in to sync

If you sign in (Sign in with Apple), we store:

  • Account data: your email address (an Apple relay address if you chose to hide it) and an account ID.
  • What you record: baby name and date of birth, custom foods, and for each meal the time, foods, texture, amount, reaction and notes.
  • Family sharing: the family you create, its members and invite codes.

Photos are not uploaded; they stay on your device.

This data is stored with Supabase in Frankfurt, Germany (EU) and encrypted in transit. It is visible only to you and the people you invite to your family. We use it only to provide sync and family sharing. We never sell or share it.

Purchases

Payments are handled entirely by Apple. We never receive your payment details.

Camera, photos and notifications

The app uses the camera or reads photos only when you take or pick them. Photos are resized and kept inside the app. The optional "suggest foods from a photo" feature runs on the device, and nothing is uploaded. Reminders are local notifications.

Tracking and ads

No ads, and no tracking, analytics or third-party data collection.

Retention and deletion

You can delete any record in the app at any time. In Settings you can leave a family or delete your account. Deleting your account deletes your account data from the cloud. If you are the last member of a family, that family's records are deleted too. Deleting the app removes its data from your device.

Your rights

You may ask to access, correct, export or delete your data, and you may complain to your local data protection authority. For users in the EU/EEA and UK, the legal basis is performance of the service you requested (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)). Reactions, symptoms and notes you record may be health data about your baby; we process them only because you chose to sign in and sync, on the basis of your explicit consent (GDPR Art. 9(2)(a)). You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting those records or your account; this does not affect processing before withdrawal. When you delete your account we also revoke the Sign in with Apple authorization.

Children

The app is meant for parents and caregivers, not for children. Parents enter and control their baby's data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
